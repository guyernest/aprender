<objective>
Measure, before the hours-long gate run, what one training step costs on this host at GoldStandard's real
row length, and turn that into the declared epoch count and an honest date.

Purpose: D-10 says the one-epoch probe runs first and replaces an estimate with a measurement; D-08 says
`--epochs` is declared from it; D-18's date is re-priced if the per-step time exceeds 3.0 s. train.py cannot
do this itself (production needs 3 seeds and 4..12 epochs; the synthetic-fixture variant would leave a run
dir and score the whole eval set), so a separate driver reuses train.train_seed and writes no run dir.

Output: `scripts/laya_train/probe_epoch.py` (+ `--dry-run`), `just laya-probe-epoch`, `10-PROBE-EVIDENCE.json`.
</objective>
