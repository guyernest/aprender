condensed annotation guide as `task.json`, measure its prefix cost and retention with the served tokenizer
against a served door that is a parameter, and put the measured trade-off in front of the owner at a blocking
checkpoint before anything trains: the guide text AND the served maximum text count.

the evidence to record, a fuller guide with a lower door as a real option next to the eight-text door (D-05,
D-06, D-15). The data rule (dedup, the five conflicting-label texts, the 20 % held-out eval) is the one thing
the gate's eval claim rests on (D-03).

the approved text), the `laya-prepare-goldstandard` recipe and the `prefix-budget` gate row, the gitignored
data dir `data/decide/goldstandard-2024/`, and `10-TASK-EVIDENCE.json` with `chosen_max_texts`.
