Turn the partner's GoldStandard2024 CSV into the Laya data dir by a rule declared before it runs, draft
the condensed annotation guide as `task.json`, measure its prefix cost with the served tokenizer, and put
the measured trade-off in front of the owner at a blocking checkpoint before anything trains.

prefix P decides how many tweets fit one call under the 8-text / 800-token door; both must be measured
and approved, never estimated (D-05, D-06). The data rule (dedup, the five conflicting-label texts, the
20 % held-out eval) is the one thing the gate's eval claim rests on (D-03).

text), the `laya-prepare-goldstandard` recipe and the `prefix-budget` gate row, the gitignored data dir
`data/decide/goldstandard-2024/`, and `10-TASK-EVIDENCE.json`.
