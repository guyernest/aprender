Review-driven revisions in this pass (2026-08-08, `/gsd:plan-phase 1 --reviews`):
- **Head boundaries.** The slice now uses 2 heads x 32 dims, not 4 x 16. Slicing hidden 64 into
  four 16-dim heads cuts MiniLM's real 32-dim heads in half, producing a synthetic attention
  structure from real numbers. Two 32-dim heads preserve two complete original head boundaries.
- **Vocabulary remapping is separated from tokenizer identity.** Fixtures carry CANONICAL HF ids
  everywhere a tokenizer boundary is described; `input_ids_slice` is an additional, clearly named
  field, and `vocab_remap.json` is the bridge the slice encoder applies internally.
- **Full-checkpoint -> APR conversion is defined**, not left to the loader to guess:
  `fetch_full_weights.py` now converts and emits a manifest carrying BOTH the source safetensors
  digest and the produced APR digest.
- **Tolerance derivation gains per-family FLOORS** so `10x observed delta` can never yield zero or
  an unrealistically tight value, and separate families for forward / reduction / gradient /
  optimizer reduction-order differences.
- **`gradients.json` gains `analytically_zero[]`** with justifications and a `parameter_order[]`
  ordered array (JSON object key order is not a stable proof of HF ordering).
- **New `activation_reference.json`** so 01-08 can gate exact-GELU parity independently of the
  per-layer FFN outputs, and **`optimizer_step.json` gains expected pre/post loss**.
- **Upstream digests are recorded and verified fail-closed** on every subsequent fetch.
