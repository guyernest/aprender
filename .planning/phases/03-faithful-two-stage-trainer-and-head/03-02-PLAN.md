<objective>
Replace the SetFit encoder's dropout mask source with a counter-based Philox construction keyed by
(root_seed, "dropout"-domain, site, FORWARD ORDINAL) so masks are replay-exact, thread-count
independent, AND independent between the pair's two siamese branches (D-15 as amended), and
empirically falsify the one quantified nondeterminism hazard on the forward path — GEMM
partitioning that depends on rayon pool size when m <= 128 (D-13, RESEARCH Pitfall 3).

Purpose: TRN-06's bitwise two-clean-runs guarantee is unbuildable while masks come from a stateful
Mutex<StdRng> (draw i depends on every prior draw; StdRng is not stable across rand versions) and
while A3 (GEMM edge-tile visibility) is an assumption instead of a measurement.
Output: dropout_rng module with the forward-ordinal coordinate + encoder integration + step/branch
threading; GEMM thread-count falsification harness at fixed pool sizes; resolved decision on the old
FNV/SplitMix site keying.
</objective>
