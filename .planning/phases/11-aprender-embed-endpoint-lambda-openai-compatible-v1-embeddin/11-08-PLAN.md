<objective>
Price the endpoint: derive every bound from the real Graviton measurement, write them as one provable contract, mirror them in Rust with equality tests, enforce them in the
right order before any expensive work, and finish the runtime shell so that a panic, a load failure, an identity mismatch, a response that is too large or a deadline that is too
close each become a well-formed answer and never a FunctionError (CONTEXT R2, R3, R5, R6).

Purpose: R5's remaining error classes are the ones the OpenAI table cannot reach: size (413), slowness, crashes and the six-megabyte response cap. They are all cheap to prevent and
expensive to discover live. The contract keeps the numbers and their reasons in one reviewable place, as the decide boundary contract did through ten versions.

Output: the contract, the derivation script with its case table, the bounds and identity modules, the finished runtime shell, and the recipe changes that keep the local binary runs working with the identity requirement.
</objective>
