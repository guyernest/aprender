  <context>
Why it matters: `usage.prompt_tokens`, the evidence that a 1,600-wordpiece chunk was cut at 256, and the endpoint's memory safety all need facts the tokenizer keeps private (SentenceBatch is built inside the crate; the only public batch producer is behind the test-only conformance-fixtures feature).
