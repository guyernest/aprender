<objective>
Declare the motivation-v1 gate run — its waiver, recipe, epochs and calibration slice — in the gate contract before anything
trains, run it exactly once (a second attempt only under the owner's written rerun authorization, never a third), and pack and
verify the result under the D-19/D-20 rules: ship a pass, ship a declared ECE-only failure labelled as a gate-failed pilot, and
halt for the owner on a margin failure or anything else.

Purpose: one declared run with every tunable fixed in advance is what makes the gate a gate; the waiver exists for this run
only and is bound to its data and recipe by sha256.

Output: the contract declaration (its own commit), the run dir and artifact (gitignored), the write-once per-attempt records, the
index 12-GATE-RUN-EVIDENCE.json, the rerun authorization when one is given, and the owner's final disposition.
</objective>
