## Options

Either way: the pack task's verify stays the authoritative eligibility check (the earlier owner decision), nothing is deployed by plan 14-21, contracts/ and crates/aprender-decide are unchanged by it, and D-16 stays open in 14-VERIFICATION human_verification.

### keep-bound: keep the bound and accept the refusals

- Consequence: no contract change. The bound stays the one every Phase 8, 10 and 12 artifact was judged by. A refusal is fail-closed and visible.
- Consequence: a gate-passing CUDA run can end with no .apr; the Graviton acceptance counts above are the observed rate. A rerun costs about an hour of A10G.
- Consequence: the cross-CPU gap stays a known, measured finding rather than a resolved one.

### amend-per-study: amend the parity contract according to this study (owner-only)

- Consequence: it removes the structural mismatch documented above (a bound derived from GPU noise judging a CPU re-score), with numbers taken from this study rather than from argument.
- Consequence: one-way. It changes what every later pack accepts and what every decide server's eligibility means. It must be argued per set (fine_tuned and zero_shot) with the Graviton spread in view.
- Consequence: this Mac and Graviton disagree on the same bytes, so the local laya-verify may need a stated role.
- Consequence: it needs its own planned change set and review; plan 14-21 contains neither.
