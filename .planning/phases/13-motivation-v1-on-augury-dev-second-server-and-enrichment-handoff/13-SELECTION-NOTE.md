# Selection note: which stored posts go to the expert (motivation-v1, D-17)

For the experiment team (the partner) and the aprender owner. This note is declared guidance. Its rule is implemented
once, in `scripts/laya_train/select_motivation.py`. The rule section below is that script's own output, so the note and
the code cannot say two different things. The script has been exercised on synthetic records only; no real post has
passed through it.

## 1. What to send and why

The recommended selection has three slices and a default budget of 300 posts. The partner may change the budget (section
2 shows how the shares scale).

- A **random control** slice: a uniformly random sample of the pool, the unbiased reference.
- An **uncertainty band** slice: posts where the model hesitates. Its band straddles the probability threshold
  experiment-coach acts on, so it probes the decisions that threshold actually makes.
- A **stratified quota** slice: posts by predicted label, with `conspiratorial` over-sampled because it is the thinnest
  class in the motivation-v1 training rows.

Sending only high-probability rows teaches the model nothing new about its own errors. The retrain gains most from the
posts where the model hesitates or is wrong, and those are rarely its most confident rows. A confident row the expert
confirms adds little; a confident row the expert overturns is exactly what the control and uncertainty slices are there
to find.

The random control slice is what makes the next comparison honest. The uncertainty and quota slices are biased by design:
they over-sample hard posts and the thin class. A rate measured on them (agreement with the expert, false assignments, a
class share) therefore does not describe the stored posts as a whole. A population estimate from those slices needs
inverse-inclusion weights, each post weighted by the inverse of its chance of being selected. The control slice needs
none.

## 1b. What the control slice can and cannot claim

The control slice is a uniform random sample of the eligible unique-text pool of this scoring round. The pool is what is
left after removing texts already in motivation-v1 (train or eval) and collapsing duplicate texts. It is therefore an
unbiased reference for that pool and for this round of scoring and annotation only:

- It says nothing about posts that were never scored, or about a later round's posts.
- It says nothing about texts that were excluded as already known.
- Once its rows are used to train a model, it is no longer an independent evaluation of that model. A later comparison
  needs a fresh control slice drawn from posts that model has not seen.

The selection file's header states exactly this in its `control_population` field.

| other | quota - conspiratorial - social_affiliative | 30 | 10 |

- **Budget:** 300 by default; any integer from 1 to 20000 (the row cap of a motivation-enrichment-v1 file). The partner may change the budget; the shares then scale by the integer formulas above.
- **Band (the uncertainty slice):** a post is band-eligible when `p1 - p2 < 0.20` or `0.45 <= p1 <= 0.75`, where p1 and p2 are its largest and second-largest probabilities, compared as exact decimals. The interval straddles experiment-coach's `motivation_min_prob` 0.6.
- **Predicted label (the quota slice):** the label of the largest probability; a tie goes to the earlier label in served order (social_affiliative, conspiratorial, other).
- **Seed:** `motivation-selection-v1` by default (`--seed` changes it, and the selection header records it).
- **Rank key:** the lower-case hex sha256 of the UTF-8 bytes of `<seed>:<post_id>`. Smaller keys are drawn first, so neither file order nor line order changes the selection.
- **Pool:** a record whose text (nfc-trim-ws-v1) is already a motivation-v1 train or eval text is excluded; records with the same text are then deduplicated, keeping the smaller rank key.
- **Draw order:** (1) control: the smallest rank keys of the whole pool; (2) uncertainty: the smallest rank keys among the band-eligible posts not yet drawn; (3) quota: for each predicted label, the smallest rank keys among that label's posts not yet drawn.
- **No post twice, no backfill:** a slice that finds too few posts takes all it finds and the header reports the shortfall; no other slice makes up the difference.

**Why the band straddles the threshold.** experiment-coach acts on a post only when its top probability reaches
`motivation_min_prob`. Posts just below the threshold are the ones it drops, and posts just above are the ones it acts
on. The band takes both sides, so the expert's labels show whether the threshold sits in the right place.

The margin clause is meant to add posts whose two top labels are close, but with three labels it adds no post in
practice. A post whose top probability is below the band's lower edge
always has a top-two margin below the margin edge, so it is already inside the band. A post whose top probability is above
the upper edge always has a margin far above the margin edge. In practice the band is therefore every post whose top
probability is at most the upper edge.

**Draw order.** The control slice is drawn first, from the whole pool, so that it stays a uniform sample. Taking the hard
posts first and drawing the control from what is left would bias it. Uncertainty comes next, and the quota is filled
from what remains.

**No backfill, shortfalls reported.** When a slice finds fewer posts than its target, for example too few posts predicted
`conspiratorial`, it takes all it finds. The selection header's `counts.shortfall` reports the gap. No other slice makes up
the difference, so every slice keeps the meaning the rule gives it, and the selection may be smaller than the budget.

**Dedupe and exclusion.** Texts are compared by the one text identity the ingest uses (nfc-trim-ws-v1: Unicode NFC, trim,
every whitespace run collapsed to one space). A stored text that equals a motivation-v1 train or eval text is excluded,
because the model was trained or evaluated on it. Two stored posts with the same text count once, keeping the smaller
rank key. The header counts both: `excluded_known_text` and `duplicate_text_skipped`.

**`band_share_of_pool`.** The header reports the share of the pool that is band-eligible. When it is near 1, the band is
not selective: almost every post qualifies, so the uncertainty slice is close to a second random sample rather than a
probe of hard posts. Read the uncertainty slice's results with that share in mind.

## 3. The record each scored post produces

Running a stored post through the motivation-v1 server produces one `motivation-record-v1` object. The format is declared
in `contracts/motivation-enrichment-v1.yaml` under `formats.motivation-record-v1`. A record has exactly these keys:

- `post_id` and `source`: the partner's own identifier and collection name.
- `text`: the text exactly as sent.
- `labels`, `probabilities`, `artifact_sha256`, `tokens` and `truncated`: copied unchanged from the classify response.
- `deployment`: the deployment that scored the post.
- `scored_at`: a UTC timestamp ending in Z, as the contract's `scored_at` pattern declares.

The deployment object is the deployment triple plus its endpoint:

- `pmcp_target` `augury-dev`;
- `aws_account` `783960389328`;
- `region` `eu-west-1`;
- `endpoint`: the one the deploy prints.

Each record file holds one deployment. Record files live in the partner repository. The aprender side reads them in place
and never copies them.

## 4. What the expert returns

The expert returns a `motivation-enrichment-v1` file, declared in the same contract under
`formats.motivation-enrichment-v1`.

- **Header:** `format`, `annotation_mode` (this round `prediction_shown`, because the expert sees the model's output),
  `artifact_sha256` and `guide`.
- **One row per annotated post:**
  - `post_id` and `text`;
  - the expert's `label`;
  - the `evidence` for the recorded function: `clear`, `weak`, `mixed` or `none`;
  - `selection_slice`: the slice this selection gave the post (`control`, `uncertainty` or `quota`), copied over unchanged.
    It is `unspecified` for a post that did not come from this selection.

The label must be the expert's own judgement and never a copied model prediction (D-03). The selection file is built to
help with this: it carries `post_id`, `selection_slice` and `rank_key` only, with no text, no probability and no label.
The annotation sheet joins it back to the record files on `post_id`.

The evidence level `none` and the three refused (label, evidence) pairs are the contract's `d15_amendment`. Until the
owner confirms them in the hand-off, they are pending, and they must be confirmed before the partner builds the sheet. The
ingest refuses those pairs as written.

## 5. A suggestion: record the label before looking at the probabilities

This is a suggestion only and not a requirement (D-16). Where the sheet layout allows, the expert can record a label
before opening the probability column. The round is `prediction_shown` either way, and the ingest records that mode and
never acts on it. Labelling first simply reduces how much the model's numbers can anchor the expert's judgement.

## 6. How to run the reference rule

```
uv run --project scripts/laya_train --frozen python -I scripts/laya_train/select_motivation.py \
    --records <record file> [--records <another record file> ...] \
    --out <selection file outside any repository> \
    [--budget N] [--seed S] [--exclude-data DIR | --no-exclude]
```

- `--records` is repeatable. The files are named `source-1`, `source-2`, ... in command-line order, and a file's name is
  never printed or written. A path inside the aprender repository is refused.
- `--out` is written once. An existing file, or a path inside the aprender repository, is refused. Write it in the partner
  repository or a scratch directory.
- `--budget` defaults to 300, and `--seed` defaults to `motivation-selection-v1`. The header records both, so anyone can
  repeat the selection from the same record files.
- **Exclusion on the partner's machine.** The default `--exclude-data` is `data/decide/motivation-v1`. That directory
  exists only on the aprender operator's machine. Anywhere else the script refuses to guess (`select-exclude-missing`), so
  there are two choices:
  - Run with `--no-exclude`. A selected post may then repeat a motivation-v1 text. Nothing is lost: when the expert's file
    comes back, the ingest's D-11 and D-12 rules still drop any eval overlap and any text that ends up with conflicting
    labels.
  - Or ask the aprender side to run the rule over the partner's record files, read in place.
- stdout carries the selection counts as one JSON line, then `SELECT OK`. A refusal prints one `REFUSED select-...` line
  naming the source id, a row number and a field, never a value.

The script was exercised on synthetic records only: made-up texts carrying a planted marker, which must never appear in
its output. It has not seen a stored post.

## 7. What this phase did not do

- No post was collected, scored or selected. No record file from the partner exists yet.
- No label was written, and no model prediction was turned into a label.
- No retrain. motivation-v1 is unchanged and stays the served model until a retrain on the expert's labels passes its own
