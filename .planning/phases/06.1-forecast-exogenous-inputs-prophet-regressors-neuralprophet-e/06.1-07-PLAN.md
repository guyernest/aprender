<objective>
Finish D-22 by making the one `RegressorArg` shape actually work on the neuralprophet arm, and
decide the one thing this phase owns that nothing else could: what happens when a regressor
meets an imputed daily grid.

Purpose: NeuralProphet trains on a daily grid denser than the caller's rows. Lag-free it never
reads an imputed day's regressor value — all four fill rules including a deliberate garbage
probe are bit-identical. With lags it reads every one of them, and two DEFENSIBLE rules
measured 10.48 apart on a series of scale 35.32, about 30 percent, while a wrong value on 12
percent of grid days flipped the sign of both coefficients. The change request's contract left
those values undefined. This plan refuses that case by name and makes the lag-free guarantee
structural rather than test-borne.

Output: the by-construction indexing, the gap refusal, the multiplicative refusal, and the
four-rule probe kept as a falsification probe beside a structural fact.
</objective>
