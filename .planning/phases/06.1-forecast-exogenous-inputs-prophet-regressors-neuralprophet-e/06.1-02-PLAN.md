<objective>
Expand the tracer's single 24-column proof into the full rung ladder D-23 requires, at both
committed widths: 24 columns (four regressors, no holidays) and 30 columns (the same four
regressors plus `blackfriday` with a `-1..+2` window and `newyear` with `+0..+1`). Data prep,
then predict-at-Python-params, then components, then the fit objective — never collapsed into
one end-to-end tolerance.

Purpose: parity over self-consistency, rung by rung. Every port in this project matched
Python because a bottom-up ladder made each assumption checkable at its own rung. The tracer
proved the path exists; this proves it is correct at the width where holidays and regressors
coexist and the column sort is load-bearing.

Output: `contracts/prophet-parity-v1.yaml` regressor equations, the second committed oracle
fixture, and `reg_parity.rs` — an in-crate `--lib` test module, because this crate ships no
integration-test targets and a new one is dark until a human edits the CI workflow.
